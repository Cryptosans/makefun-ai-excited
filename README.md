A vintage steam locomotive smashes out of its own travel poster. The framed print hanging on a station wall splits open as the train charges straight through it — shredded paper in the air, steam and sparks bursting around it, the headlamp glaring right at the viewer — while two travellers throw themselves clear. The poster stops being a picture and becomes the journey: the train in the frame refuses to stay flat on the wall and comes roaring out at you instead.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` (a 1024×1024 JPEG) into the output directory. Check it with:

```sh
node test/run-check.mjs
```
